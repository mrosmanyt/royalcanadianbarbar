About Us

Your Neighbourhood Beauty Store & Barbershop

Royal Canadian Barber is a beauty products store and men's barbershop in Mississauga, Ontario, proudly owned and run by Erum Shahzadi. We stock a wide range of professional beauty and hair-care products, and our barbers deliver sharp, precise cuts and classic grooming for men and boys of every age.

Whether you're stopping by to pick up your favourite hair tools and skincare, or booking a fresh cut and beard trim, our friendly team is here to take care of you.
